Author presentation

Conrad Myers writes fiction across two lanes: grounded adult erotic stories and report-driven military sci-fi. The common thread is a direct, practical style that prioritizes atmosphere, tension, and characters with clear wants.

The adult work is written for adults. It leans into intimacy and psychology: people, impulses, and the situations that push them into choices they didn’t plan to make. It aims to feel human, close, and honest, even when the characters aren’t.

The sci-fi work is structured like internal documentation: briefings, field notes, threat grading, and pragmatic observations. It’s meant to read like an archive you weren’t supposed to access, where details matter and the voice is controlled, operational, and a little cold.

This site is intentionally minimal. Each title has its own panel with a short description and a direct link to its product page. New releases will appear at the top.

IMC XENOBIOLOGICAL RECON ARCHIVE
Compendium of Extraterrestrial Megafauna

A military reconnaissance archive from the IMC, documenting the dominant megafauna of planet VBBS-8993.

Written as an internal field compendium, this volume reads like a classified briefing: taxonomy notes, threat-tier assessments, behavior patterns, habitat and range observations, anatomical details, and exploitation considerations. It’s deliberately text-forward, built for readers who want “report style” worldbuilding and hard-edged military tone rather than illustrations.

Format: text-heavy archive Tone: operational / clinical Focus: extraterrestrial megafauna

The Lonely Taxi Driver

A tense, grounded adult story about a man who can’t stop feeling the emptiness after the engine turns off.

Alex is 36, works long shifts, and drives home to silence. He’s straight, touch-starved, and tired of pretending he isn’t. Between late-night pickups, awkward conversations, and choices made in moments of weakness, he starts looking for relief in places that don’t match the life he thought he’d have. It’s a story about loneliness first and desire second, told with a close, blunt voice.
