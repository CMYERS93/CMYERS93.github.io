Author presentation

Conrad Myers writes fiction across two lanes: grounded adult erotic stories and report-driven military sci-fi. The common thread is a straightforward style that prioritizes atmosphere, tension, and characters with clear wants.

The erotic work leans into intimacy and psychology: people, impulses, and the situations that push them into choices they didn’t plan to make. It’s adult fiction, written for adults, without trying to pretend it’s anything else.

The sci-fi work is structured like internal documentation: briefings, field notes, threat grading, and pragmatic observations. It aims to feel like a real archive you weren’t meant to see, where the details matter and the voice is controlled, practical, and a little cold.

This site is intentionally minimal. Each book gets a full-width panel with a short description and a direct link to its Gumroad product page.

IMC XENOBIOLOGICAL RECON ARCHIVE
Compendium of Extraterrestrial Megafauna

A military reconnaissance archive from the IMC, documenting the dominant megafauna of planet VBBS-8993.

Written as an internal field compendium, this volume reads like a classified briefing: taxonomy notes, threat-tier assessments, behavior patterns, habitat and range observations, anatomical details, and exploitation considerations. It’s deliberately text-forward, built for readers who want “report style” worldbuilding and hard-edged military tone rather than illustrations.

Format: text-heavy archive Tone: military sci-fi Focus: extraterrestrial megafauna
